Latest Developments

  • • Nuclear: Oklo’s Groves Isotope Test Reactor reached criticality (DOE, Aug. 6, 2026) — the fifth DOE-authorized advanced reactor this summer under the Reactor Pilot Program. This is a zero-power test unit in Lockhart, TX, not the Aurora commercial plant.
  • • AI + Nuclear R&D: DOE Genesis Mission (July 22) selected 278 projects; the largest is a 3-year, $60M nuclear-AI award. DOE also announced more than $800M in consortium partner commitments. Selections are for award negotiations, not final funding.
  • • Semiconductors: Commerce signed 7 CHIPS R&D letters of intent on July 29 for $874M (photonics, memory, packaging, substrates). LOIs are not final awards; Commerce takes a minority, non-controlling equity stake if funded.
  • • Critical Minerals: July 30 Presidential Determination under DPA §101 on recoverable CMMs (black mass, end-of-life REE magnets, scrap). BIS temporary final rule (Aug. 6) requires, as of Aug. 27, 100% of monthly black-mass and tungsten scrap sales to U.S. persons unless BIS grants an exception.
  • • Quantum: $2.013B CHIPS Act LOIs (May 21, 2026) for 9 companies remain letters of intent, not closed awards. June 22 EO 14413 still directs a utility-scale QC-ADDS machine for a DOE facility; DOE partnership-model report is due within 180 days of the order.
  • • Large nuclear: DOE EDF issued a $17.5B conditional loan commitment (June 23) for long-lead items on up to 10 Westinghouse AP1000 units. Separate Oct. 2025 partnership targets at least $80B of Westinghouse reactor construction — that figure is project value, not a cash grant.
