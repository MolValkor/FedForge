How FedForge works

Sources and method, in four steps.

  1. STEP 1

    Start from the official notice

    Department of War / DoD contract announcements, DOE, NIST / CHIPS, the Office of Strategic Capital, White House actions, and USAspending.gov prime-award records.

  2. STEP 2

    Tag the status

    FINAL, CONDITIONAL, LOI or UCA. A letter of intent or a UCA ceiling is not cash out the door. Glossary.

  3. STEP 3

    Quote the dollars as sourced

    Amounts come from the source. Where a notice separates money obligated from face value or a ceiling, both are shown, and every entry links to the notice.

  4. STEP 4

    Ticker only when verified

    A ticker is attached only when the recipient is a verified public company or a named subsidiary. Private recipients stay labelled private. The list is not padded.

Not investment advice. FedForge is an information tool. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Award amounts can be ceilings, and historical close-to-close returns are not forecasts. Always read the official source. How to read a federal award →
