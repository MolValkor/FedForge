Follow new federal awards

FedForge publishes an RSS feed of the awards it tracks. Each item has the recipient, the ticker when the recipient is a verified public company, the status (FINAL, CONDITIONAL, LOI or UCA), the dollar amount as the source states it, and the official source link. New items appear in your reader when this site's data is refreshed.

1. Copy the feed address

https://thefedforge.com/feed.xml

Or open it directly: feed.xml.

2. Paste it into a feed reader

Any RSS reader works. Most have a "+ Add" or "Subscribe" button that accepts a URL. Common free options include Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire (Mac / iPhone), Thunderbird, and the RSS features built into many email and news apps. FedForge is not affiliated with any of them.

Slack users can also add the feed to a channel with Slack's RSS app.

Also useful

  • Watchlist: star the tickers you care about. It is saved only in this browser (no account), and the dashboard can filter to your watched names.
  • Awards: every award card has a "Copy link" button so you can share one specific award.
  • Free briefing: a printable one-page summary. No email gate.
