How to use this site

  1. Start on the dashboard one-liner, then the sourced developments strip.
  2. Open Awards. Filter nuclear / magnets / chips / defense.
  3. Read the status tag before the dollar: FINAL (agreement or paid), CONDITIONAL (loan/commitment still gated), LOI (letter of intent), UCA (undefinitized contract action — ceiling is not obligated).
  4. Click Official source. If a ticker is missing, we did not have a verified U.S.-listed recipient.

Nuclear / SMRs

Three different instruments, do not mash them together:

  • $17.5B DOE EDF — conditional loans for AP1000 long-lead items via ANDP HOLDCO (Westinghouse sponsor). Private recipient.
  • Centrus / ACO $900M FFP (about $1.07B with options) — HALEU task order, ticker LEU, Jul 1.
  • PG&E $271M paid — first Civil Nuclear Credit payment for Diablo Canyon, ticker PCG, Aug 14.

Oklo Groves criticality (Aug 6) is a DOE Reactor Pilot Program authorization — useful context, not a FedForge award row. Genesis Mission $60M nuclear-AI selection is still negotiation.

Magnets / rare earths

  • USA Rare Earth CHIPS mine-to-magnet (Jun 3): up to $277M + up to $1.3B loan. Ticker USAR.
  • Niron Magnetics OSC $150M conditional (Aug 7). Private.
  • DPA determination + BIS black-mass/tungsten allocation (Jul 30 / Aug 27) change who can buy scrap. No recipient dollar on those pages, so they live under Pipeline.

The 2025 MP Materials DoD package is real but pre-window — not duplicated as a new Sep 2026 award.

Semiconductors / CHIPS

  • Definitive, private: SandboxAQ $500M, I-Pulse $250M SiC, Bosch up to $225M SiC.
  • LOI: $874M seven-company R&D package (Jul 29). Verified ticker: GFS up to $300M. Aeluma is named; we did not verify a ticker, so none is shown.
  • TSMC private FDI is excluded. May 21 $2.013B quantum LOIs are pre-window and not in this snapshot. IonQ was never on that NIST list.

Defense tech (only with a federal notice)

LMT THAAD UCA and PAC-3 MSE modification; RTX Tomahawk $22.9B and AMRAAM UCA. Read obligated vs face value. This site is not a general defense-stock blog.
