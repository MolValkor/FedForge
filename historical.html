Historical pattern

Case studies, not forecasts, and not a reason to treat any ticker on this site as a recommendation.

1958–1970s

NASA and the space race

NASA’s peak budget share funded aerospace, computing, and materials primes. The lesson we actually use: follow named contracts, not slogans.

1960s–1990s

ARPANET to commercial internet

DARPA-funded networking research became infrastructure decades later. That lag is why this site separates LOI / UCA / CONDITIONAL from FINAL paid.

WWII – Cold War

Bell Labs, semiconductors, nuclear

Military and government demand for transistors, plus Manhattan Project / naval nuclear programs, seeded later commercial industries. Pattern, not a price target.
