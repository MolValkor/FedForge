NUCLEAR ENERGY & FUSION

Nuclear + AI Power

DOE Reactor Pilot Program: five advanced reactors reached criticality this summer, including Oklo Groves (Aug. 6). Genesis Mission added a 3-year, $60M nuclear-AI selection (July 22). DOE EDF: $17.5B conditional AP1000 long-lead loans (June 23). DOE identified 16 federal sites (April 2025) for AI infrastructure with nuclear generation option.

Key Tickers: OKLO, SMR, BWXT, CEG, VST, LEU
Momentum: Very High (AI demand + policy support)
QUANTUM COMPUTING

Quantum Computing & QIS

$2.013B CHIPS LOIs (May 21, 2026; 9 companies, not closed awards) + EO 14413 (June 22) for a DOE-hosted large-scale quantum computer (QC-ADDS). Recipients on the NIST list: IBM, GFS, Atom, Diraq, D-Wave, Infleqtion, PsiQuantum, Quantinuum, Rigetti — not IonQ.

Key Tickers: IBM, GFS, RGTI, QBTS
Momentum: Extremely High
AI INFRASTRUCTURE

AI Compute & Data Centers

DOE Genesis Mission (July 22): 278 AI-for-science selections and $800M+ partner commitments. April 2025 DOE RFI listed 16 federal sites for data-center / energy co-location. Power remains the bottleneck; nuclear offtakes (e.g., Oklo–Meta 1.2 GW Ohio) are private demand, not federal grants.

Key Tickers: VST, CEG, NVDA, AVGO, power utilities
Momentum: Extremely High
SEMICONDUCTORS

Semiconductors & CHIPS Act

Newest official package: $874M CHIPS R&D LOIs (July 29) for compute-supply-chain R&D. GAO (report GAO-26-109121) said as of April 2026 Commerce had 49 projects across 24 companies and had disbursed $13.1B of $31.5B in awards. Prior site claim of >$645B private investment could not be confirmed on an official page this pass.

Key Tickers: INTC, TSM, AMAT, KLAC, LRCX, ASML, GFS
Momentum: High
CRITICAL MINERALS

Critical Minerals & Magnets

Project Vault ($10B EXIM loan, Feb. 2026) + MP Materials DoD package ($400M equity + $150M loan + offtake + price floor, 2025). New since July 13: DPA determination (July 30) and BIS allocation order (effective Aug. 27) on black mass and tungsten scrap.

Key Tickers: MP, LAC, rare earth plays
Momentum: Extremely High
DEFENSE TECH
