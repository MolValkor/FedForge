EXECUTIVE ACTION July 30, 2026 · effective Aug 27

DPA Determination + BIS Allocation on Recoverable CMMs

Presidential Determination under DPA §101 authorizes Commerce action on recoverable critical minerals and materials (black mass, end-of-life rare-earth magnets, swarf, scrap; copper scrap excluded). BIS temporary final rule requires 100% of monthly black-mass and tungsten scrap sales to U.S. persons from Aug. 27, 2026, unless BIS grants an exception. Comments invited on whether additional sales requirements are needed.

Sectors impacted: Critical Minerals, Defense, Battery recycling
