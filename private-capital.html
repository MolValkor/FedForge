Sectors · private capital

Private capital building federal capacity

Company money, not appropriations, going into U.S. shipyards, rocket-motor plants, drone factories, nuclear fuel, magnets, chips and launch sites that the Navy, the Department of War, DOE and NASA will rely on. Anduril's $3.7B submarine shipyard in Maryland is the newest.

Every card links to its source. Amounts are quoted as the source states them; "Undisclosed" means no source we found gives a number. Where federal money is attached (a contract, loan or grant), it is shown separately with its own source. Tickers appear only for companies that are themselves listed.

These are company announcements, not federal awards, and plans can change. Not investment advice. Last reviewed Oct 7, 2026. Methodology

Methodology

  • What qualifies. A company (private or listed) puts its own capital into a U.S. facility the federal government will depend on: shipyards, munitions and rocket motors, drone and autonomy factories, nuclear fuel and plants, rare-earth magnets and critical minerals, semiconductor fabs, space launch, and grid or data centers serving defense. Announced in roughly the last 24 months (Oct 7, 2024 to the review date).
  • Sources required. Every entry carries at least one link to a company or government press release or a reputable outlet. Each fact on a card (amount, status, ticker, federal link) points to one of those links. No source, no entry.
  • Amounts. Quoted as the source words them ("more than", "up to", "over five years"). If no source states a figure, the card says Undisclosed. We do not add up announcements, estimate totals, or add growth rates.
  • Federal money. Contracts, loans, grants and offtakes are listed separately from the private amount, with their own source. A conditional loan is labeled conditional.
  • Tickers. Only for companies that are themselves exchange-listed, with a source. Private companies have none. A listed partner is named only when the source names it.
  • Status. Announced, under construction, or operating, only when a source says so, and as of that source's date. Otherwise no status is shown.
  • FedForge lane. Each entry maps to an existing lane: Nuclear, Magnets / rare earths, Semiconductors / CHIPS, or Defense.
  • Last reviewed: 2026-10-07. The data file is data/private-capital.json, checked by tools/check_private_capital.py.
